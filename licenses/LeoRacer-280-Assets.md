# Leo Racer 2.8.0 scenery and vehicle audio credits

The supplied combustion recordings were prepared for Leo Racer with the user's permission to bundle them. The vehicle audio manifest lists source paths, source hashes, and processed WAV hashes. The existing soundtrack keeps its separate credits.

The recorded tire loop is “Car tire squeal skid loop,” recorded by Tom Haigh (audible-edge) and adapted by qubodup. Leo Racer resamples it and uses a filtered variant for scrub. [Source](https://lpc.opengameart.org/content/car-tire-squeal-skid-loop) · [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)

Kandy contains baked models from Bukkbeek's Landu Kelae, Vehera Vihara, and Stone Blocks collections. The user confirmed permission to publish the baked game assets on November 21, 2026. The Temple of the Tooth base GLB was supplied by the user. [Ceylon Blocks](https://bukkbeek.itch.io/ceylonblocks)

Leo Racer's grass and water use project shaders. EmacE Art’s linked shaders served as visual references. [Vegetation](https://store.godotengine.org/asset/emace-art/godot-stylized-vegetation-wind-shader/) · [Water](https://store.godotengine.org/asset/emace-art/stylized-water-shader/)
