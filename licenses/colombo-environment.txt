Colombo visual environment, October 2026

Premade building and street-prop source authors/licenses remain listed in
colombo-buildings.txt. Changes: audited component roles, metre-scale socketed
assemblies, district layout, material roughness and terracotta roof treatment.
UVs and source material atlas retained. Original downloaded sources are not shipped.

Terrain authoring is adapted from bsubard's MIT-licensed procedural terrain demo,
commit 71ff8c816839a8e45c1900c37d1671286371ed89. Full copyright and MIT notice:
bsubard-terrain-MIT.txt. Baked terrain is finite and offline; no demo controller,
mountain generator, infinite runtime streaming or demo collision is installed.

Distant Colombo-inspired visual panorama: generated using the built-in imagegen
tool on 23 October 2026, native 1774x887. No external photograph pixels embedded.
It is an artistic backdrop rather than captured or surveyed geographic data.
Lighting EXR: separately authored linear procedural atmosphere and sun radiance.
The generated PNG is not presented as measured HDR illumination.

Existing Lotus Tower and custom architecture retain their original attribution.
Existing OpenStreetMap development data attribution remains in the shipped notices.
