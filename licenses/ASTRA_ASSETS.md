# Astra additions

Five vehicles and Shaven Leo were authored procedurally in Blender for Leo Racer, with no downloaded vehicle/character geometry. Source: `blender/build_astra_vehicles.py` and `assets/models/astra/source/*.blend`. These original assets are dedicated under CC0-1.0. Hashes and metadata: `docs/qa-astra/model-audit.json`.

Three road-decal PNGs were generated with image_gen for this project. Exact prompts: `assets/art/astra/prompts.json`. They depict Leo Racer, Colombo Grand Prix and Lotus Coast festival artwork. No photographic person reference was used for Shaven Leo.

Five original synthesized arcade cues were generated by `tools/make_astra_audio.py`: selection, lap, charge ready, tuk-tuk horn and bus horn. The generator and level/hash records accompany the project. These original synthesized cues are dedicated under CC0-1.0.

The previous eight billboard campaigns, supplied tropical foliage and authentic Lotus Tower retain their provenance in `SRILANKA_ASSETS.md` and the original source manifest. Existing third-party licenses remain applicable.
