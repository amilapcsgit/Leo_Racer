# Leo Racer 2.8.0

- Adaptive grass and water across Astra Lotus Coast, Colombo City Grand Prix, Kandy Lake Circuit, and Wennappuwa Street Circuit.
- Environment Detail offers Auto, Low, Medium, and High. Auto targets 60 FPS by tuning scenery detail during play.
- Kandy has structured terracotta roofs, Walakulu Bemma promenade modules, varied Landu Kelae trees, and additional temple courtyard details.
- Recorded RPM and load layers drive combustion engine audio. Tire recordings blend from scrub to squeal as grip breaks.
- The existing soundtrack and the 21-car roster play offline. Wennappuwa is bundled as an unranked circuit.

Use the Windows installer or extract the portable archive into a new folder and run LeoRacer.exe. Use Play Compatibility.cmd for OpenGL.
