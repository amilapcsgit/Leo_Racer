# 2.7.1 license audit — publication pending

This audit records the rights and checks for the 2.7.1 candidate.

- `LICENSE` contains the proposed custom license for original work only. The
  earlier root AGPL text is preserved byte-for-byte in
  `licenses/AGPL-3.0-legacy.txt`; previously granted rights remain in effect.
- `licenses/LeoRacer-MIT.txt` contains an existing MIT grant to Leo Racer
  contributors. Do not imply that the new license revokes that grant or the
  existing right to sell previously MIT-licensed material.
- Kenney and other included materials have their own licenses and notices.
  Their terms continue to apply; confirm all assets in the actual release.
- The project owner confirmed on 4 November 2026 that they have the necessary
  permissions to distribute the recognizable third-party character and vehicle
  designs used by Leo Racer. This confirmation is recorded as supplied by the
  owner; the underlying grants were not independently reviewed here.
- The Rokojori Public Indie License permits indie game publication but has
  restrictions on standalone distribution, toolkits and use in game mods.
  The workshop is launched through the game's executable, while its separate
  pack contains the workshop scripts rather than the library source. The
  project owner confirmed on 4 November 2026 that they have permission from
  Rokojori covering this arrangement. The grant was not independently reviewed.
  See the authoritative license at
  https://rokojori.com/en/labs/rokojori-action-library/license.
- `licenses/MODULAR_ASSETS.md` identifies CC BY and CC BY-SA material; retain
  the attribution, source details and the share-alike terms for the hovercar
  derivative. The project license does not override these licenses.
- Existing branches, tags, release binaries, release descriptions and release
  assets have not yet been fully archived or verified. Preserve public refs and
  releases until a complete verified private archive and local backup exist.

Keep the original notices in the shipped package. Publication remains contingent
on completing the verified archive and release validation.
