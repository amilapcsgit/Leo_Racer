# Leo Racer

The language of a local race and its results.

## Language

**Finish order**:
The order in which racers cross their final valid checkpoint. Equal displayed finish times do not make separate crossings the same finish.
_Avoid_: Roster order, timestamp order

**Unranked race**:
A race that can be played and completed but is ineligible for personal records. Wennappuwa and races modified by a pause-time car change are unranked.
_Avoid_: Invalid race
